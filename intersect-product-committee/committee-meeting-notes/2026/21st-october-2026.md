## Updates

* **Review actions and updates**
  * The Secretary confirmed that the UNDP contract remained unsigned while the organisation finalised its wallet arrangements, and the Committee supported setting 2 November as a deadline before reconsidering alternative proposers.
* **Review and discuss the status of milestone PRs submitted by proposers**
  * The Committee reviewed outstanding milestone submissions and identified missing reviews across several projects, emphasising the need to prioritise Milestone 1 approvals to enable payments to proposers.
  * The Chair clarified the GitHub review process, supported the assignment of reviewers directly to PRs and encouraged members to complete their reviews by the end of the week.
* **Review TSC KPI Pull Request**
  * The Chair presented feedback on the TSC KPI Pull Request, identifying discrepancies in Plutus growth projections and assumptions about the protocol revenue required to replace treasury reserve distributions.
  * The Committee discussed the gap between Cardano's sustainability targets and current adoption, highlighting the importance of a coordinated strategy, clearer budget priorities and actionable plans to achieve the 2030 objectives.
  * The TSC representative agreed to share the technical feedback with the relevant contributors, while the Committee was encouraged to submit further comments directly through the PR.
* **Discuss the process for defining 2027 objectives for Pillar 1**
  * **Review existing CIPs to identify potential infrastructure objectives**
    * The Committee discussed using existing CIPs and proposed protocol improvements as a starting point to identify infrastructure development priorities aligned with Pillar 1 of the Cardano 2030 Strategy.
  * **Engage community stakeholders to identify infrastructure needs**
    * The Committee discussed consulting ecosystem developers, infrastructure providers and other relevant stakeholders to identify technical limitations and capabilities needed to support Cardano adoption.
  * **Incorporate findings from ongoing product research**
    * The Chair suggested leveraging findings from the ongoing product research initiatives to identify infrastructure limitations and development opportunities relevant to future objectives.
  * **Explore translating identified needs into CPSs or CIPs**
    * The Committee discussed how identified needs could inform new Cardano Problem Statements or be developed into technical improvement proposals with support from engineering teams.
  * **Continue the discussion in the next session**
    * The Secretary proposed returning to the topic in the following session to further develop the process for defining 2027 infrastructure objectives, while maintaining milestone reviews as the immediate priority.
