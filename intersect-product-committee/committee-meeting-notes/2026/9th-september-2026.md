## Updates

* **Review actions and updates**
  * **Confirm cancellation of sessions in the last week of September and first week of October**
    * The Secretary confirmed the cancellation of sessions during the last week of September and first week of October to accommodate quarterly activities and provide committee members with dedicated focus time.
  * **Review committee feedback received (6 of 7 committees)**
    * The Secretary reported that six of seven committees had submitted feedback, which the Committee will review in an upcoming session.
  * **Review outstanding UNDP contract requirements**
    * The Secretary confirmed that the UNDP contract remains pending receipt of the organisation's wallet and physical addresses, required to finalise the agreement and enable payments.
  * **Confirm next Tuesday's focus group session**
    * The Secretary reminded members of the upcoming focus group session to further explore areas identified through the committee effectiveness survey.
* **Review milestone PRs submitted by proposers**
  * **Clarify when proposers can submit subsequent milestones**
    * The Committee discussed submissions for parallel milestones and agreed that subsequent milestones should be submitted after the preceding milestone has been approved.
  * **Agree timeframe for milestone reviews**
    * The Secretary encouraged reviewers to complete their initial reviews within the following week where possible, noting that milestone submissions and supporting evidence should be included directly in GitHub as Markdown to facilitate review and payment processing.
* **Prisma presentation**
  * The Committee received a presentation from Prisma outlining its regional market research project, including the initial research design, selection of target regions and planned field research to inform Cardano market-entry strategies.
  * The Committee confirmed that the project's initial deliverables and supporting evidence should be submitted directly through the GitHub PR for review, with feedback provided through the established milestone review process.
* **Q4 planning**
  * The Secretary reviewed progress against Q3 objectives and facilitated a discussion on Q4 priorities, including continued review of product research milestones, upcoming final reports and consolidation of research findings.
  * The Committee discussed reviewing feedback on Cardano KPIs, submitting proposed changes where appropriate and considering an Info Action if substantial changes are identified.
  * The Committee also discussed the importance of coordination between research and implementation activities, improved visibility of ecosystem funding and the potential contribution of product research findings to 2027 planning.
  * The Secretary confirmed that the quarterly plan would be updated for review at the next session.
