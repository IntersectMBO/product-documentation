## Updates

* **Review actions and updates**
  * **Confirm the status of the UNDP contract**
    * The Secretary confirmed that the outstanding information had been received from UNDP, with the contract expected to be issued for signing that day.
  * The Secretary also reminded members that Intersect Board elections were underway and encouraged participation in the voting process.
* **Review milestone PRs submitted by proposers**
  * The Committee reviewed progress on milestone submissions, clarified that primary and secondary reviewers can submit feedback independently, and confirmed that approved milestones should be communicated to the Secretary to initiate the payment process.
  * The Chair reiterated the requirement for proposers to include milestone deliverables and supporting evidence directly in GitHub as Markdown to ensure a permanent, verifiable public record.
  * The Committee discussed Prisma's concerns regarding submission requirements, and the proposer agreed to include the required deliverables in the repository.
* **Review Q3 progress and finalise Q4 planning**
  * The Secretary reviewed Q3 achievements, including progress on product research contracts and milestone reviews, committee feedback on KPIs, and planning documents for future Cardano upgrades.
  * The Committee reviewed Q4 priorities, including completing research milestone reviews, reviewing final reports, consolidating research learnings, finalising KPI feedback and potentially supporting the definition of 2027 deliverables.
  * The Chair proposed collecting community feedback on priorities for the Cardano upgrade following Dijkstra, including relevant CIPs and CPSs, and the Committee agreed to plan an engagement process during Q4.
* **Review Product Research Milestone Acceptance Form (MAF)**
  * The Secretary presented the Product Research Milestone 2 acceptance form, confirming that the RFP publication, proposal evaluation and contracting requirements had been met, with six of seven winning proposals contracted.
  * The Chair reviewed the acceptance criteria and raised no objections, allowing the document to proceed through procurement review and signing.
* **Review TSC KPI Pull Request**
  * The Secretary confirmed receipt of the TSC's KPI Pull Request, and the Committee agreed that members would review it asynchronously and submit comments before discussing it in a future session.
  * The Committee aimed to complete the review and merge the PR during October, subject to feedback and availability.
* **Coordinate attendance and engagement at Token2049**
  * The Committee discussed opportunities for members attending Token2049 in Singapore to meet with representatives from other Intersect committees and exchange updates on ongoing activities.
  * The Secretary proposed coordinating attendance through the Intersect committee communication channels to explore an informal cross-committee gathering during the event.
