## Updates

* **Review actions and updates**
  * **Review outstanding UNDP wallet address and contract status**
    * The Committee confirmed that the UNDP contract remained pending receipt of the required wallet address, with no response received, and discussed following up through existing contacts.
* **Review milestone PRs submitted by proposers**
  * The Chair reviewed the status of submitted research milestones and clarified the GitHub review process, including the requirement for reviewers to formally approve or request changes rather than only leave comments.
  * The Committee identified outstanding reviews across several proposals, including Nextrium, Token Infrastructure, Elk, WADA and Prisma, and discussed following up with assigned reviewers.
  * The Committee also discussed using AI to support milestone assessments and proposed creating a short instructional video to help members complete GitHub reviews correctly.
* **Review TSC KPI Pull Request**
  * The Chair confirmed that the review of the TSC KPI Pull Request would likely resume after Token2049 due to existing commitments and availability.
* **Confirm quarterly reporting and upcoming meeting schedule**
  * The Committee confirmed that the Chair and Alexa C. would present the quarterly progress report at the upcoming Intersect Steering Committee session, using the presentation prepared by the Secretary.
  * The Committee confirmed that the next two regular sessions would be cancelled to accommodate quarterly activities and Token2049, with members continuing milestone reviews asynchronously before resuming meetings on 14 October.
