## Updates

* **Review actions and updates**
  * **Review latest developments regarding the UNDP contract**
    * The Secretary reported that UNDP remained unable to complete contracting due to outstanding wallet and KYB requirements, and the Committee discussed allowing work to begin at the proposer's risk, subject to confirmation from Intersect's legal and procurement teams.
* **Review and discuss the status of milestone PRs submitted by proposers**
  * The Committee reviewed outstanding milestone submissions, confirmed that Token Infrastructure and Elk Milestone 1 had received the required approvals to progress towards payment, and discussed remaining reviews required for other projects.
  * **Address Alexandre Maaza's request for support with the WADA milestone review**
    * The Committee agreed that James Meidinger would take over the WADA review from Alexandre Maaza due to capacity constraints, while Juan Sierra offered to support the outstanding Nextream review.
* **Discuss the process for defining 2027 objectives for Pillar 1**
  * **Prepare an initial draft of objectives using existing CIPs and product research findings**
    * The Chair confirmed that an initial draft of Pillar 1 infrastructure objectives would be developed using existing CIPs and relevant research findings, with the discussion continuing in the next session and a target to progress the work by the end of November.
* **Review TSC KPI Pull Request**
  * **Review outstanding feedback from Leandros**
    * The Committee confirmed that feedback had been submitted on the TSC KPI Pull Request and that Leandros had acknowledged the comments, with a detailed response still pending.
* **Confirm suitability of the regular committee meeting time**
  * The Committee discussed the impact of daylight saving time changes on members in different time zones and agreed to revisit the meeting schedule in the following session to confirm its suitability, particularly for members based in the United States.
